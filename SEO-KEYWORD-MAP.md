# NextVisa SEO Keyword Map & Content Architecture

## Positioning
NextVisa helps travelers from Kazakhstan understand visa requirements, prepare applications, and plan international travel. Do not claim guaranteed approvals, official government status, or verified credentials unless the owner confirms them.

## Primary search intent groups
1. **Transactional** — visa service, visa consultation, help preparing documents, application support.
2. **Destination-specific** — Kazakhstan residents applying for Canada/USA/UK/Australia/Schengen/Japan/South Korea/China/New Zealand.
3. **Informational** — requirements, documents, application steps, fees, processing times, common mistakes, interview preparation.
4. **Local** — visa service in Shymkent / Kazakhstan; local contact and consultation only where factual business details are confirmed.
5. **Travel/tours** — Australia tours, gold prospecting tours, VIP/private trips; publish only after offerings and operators are verified.

## Country page template
Each destination page must have:
- Clear H1: visa destination + audience/location where appropriate.
- Short direct answer: who needs a visa and what NextVisa can assist with (avoid definitive legal advice).
- Official government/consulate source links, date checked, and a note to re-check rules.
- Eligibility overview (only verified).
- Document checklist (specific, sourced, and caveated).
- Step-by-step process.
- Official fee and service fee separated; service fee only when owner confirms price.
- Processing time caveat: government estimates can change and are not guarantees.
- FAQs based on real questions.
- Related destination links.
- CTA: WhatsApp/Telegram/contact form using verified links.
- Editorial metadata: author/reviewer and last-updated date.

## SEO/GEO publishing quality checklist
- One page, one main intent; avoid doorway pages and keyword stuffing.
- Unique helpful copy, not spun/duplicated across destinations.
- Answer common questions near the top in plain language.
- Use accurate page titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, and descriptive headings.
- Link to primary official sources and name the source.
- Keep facts and contact details consistent.
- Add appropriate schema only for truthful visible content.
- Track impressions, clicks, CTR, leads, and conversions; never promise rankings.
