# NextVisa Brand & UX Copy Guide

## Positioning
NextVisa is a visa-support and travel-planning service that helps travelers understand the process, organize information, and prepare thoughtfully. The brand should feel premium, calm, credible, and international—not flashy or fear-based.

## Audience
People planning international travel who feel overwhelmed by requirements, paperwork, and uncertainty. Avoid treating every visitor as a guaranteed visa applicant; first qualify purpose, destination, and timeline.

## Brand voice
- Calm and professional
- Clear, human, concise
- Confident but never overpromising
- Helpful, not pushy
- Transparent about official decision-makers, government charges, and agency service charges

## Primary homepage copy (RU)
**Headline:** Мир открыт. Ваш путь начинается.
**Subhead:** Визовая поддержка и путешествия с вниманием к деталям. Помогаем разобраться в процессе, подготовить документы и уверенно планировать поездку.
**CTA:** Получить консультацию
**Secondary:** Выбрать направление

**CTA:** Book a consultation
**Secondary:** Explore destinations

## Trust claims—only use when substantiated
Do not publish statements such as “98% approval,” “guaranteed visa,” “10 years of experience,” “official partner,” “thousands of clients,” or testimonials unless evidence and authorization exist. Prefer process-based statements that can be truthfully delivered.

## CTA behavior
- Primary CTA opens a configured contact channel or short form.
- No placeholder links in production.
- Add privacy notice and consent before personal information submission.
- Track CTA clicks, but never send personal data to analytics.

## Visual rules
- Midnight navy #101B2D for authority
- Champagne gold #D6B46A as a restrained accent
- Warm ivory #F5F3EE for editorial pages
- Large typography and photography with generous whitespace
- Avoid gradients, excessive icons, crowded cards, stock “visa stamp” clichés, and aggressive countdowns
- Mobile: CTA visible, navigation concise, readable type and tap targets

## Final production gate
Brand copy must be translated and reviewed by a fluent human before launch; contact channels, legal entity details, service pricing, privacy notice, and terms must be verified.
