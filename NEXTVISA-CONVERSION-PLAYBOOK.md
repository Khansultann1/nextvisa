# NextVisa Conversion Playbook — Draft

## Primary objective
Turn search and social visitors into qualified visa consultations, not just pageviews.

## Minimum conversion path
1. Visitor lands on a country/visa page.
2. Sees a clear promise limited to legitimate service (consultation, document organization, process explanation; no guaranteed approvals).
3. Understands the service, who it is for, what is included, price or price-request method, and what happens next.
4. Chooses WhatsApp, Telegram, or a short form.
5. CRM stores source, destination, travel date, contact preference, and consent.
6. Manager follows up with a defined SLA and next-step status.

## Landing page content order
- Clear country + service title
- One-sentence benefit and disclaimer that decisions belong to authorities
- 3–5 specific service inclusions
- Process in 3 steps
- Documents: explain that checklist depends on applicant and destination; link official source
- Transparent service price or “request a quote”
- FAQs
- Trust proof only when genuine (reviews, experience, real team, cases with consent)
- One primary CTA, one secondary CTA

## Lead form: keep it short
Required initially: name, phone/contact, destination, approximate travel date, preferred channel. Ask detailed sensitive documents only after explaining purpose and securing appropriate privacy protections.

## Follow-up flow
- Immediately: confirmation and what happens next
- First human contact: clarify trip purpose/date and service fit
- Send tailored checklist after qualification
- Explain price and scope
- Confirm payment and service start
- Keep status updates and close with feedback request

## Metrics
- CTA clicks / unique landing-page visitors
- Form completion / form start
- Qualified leads / total leads
- Response time
- Consultation booked / qualified lead
- Paid clients / consultations
- Revenue and margin by destination

## Rules
- Never promise visa approval or fabricate testimonials/cases
- Keep official fees separate from agency service fees
- Verify legal/privacy requirements before collecting passport or sensitive data
- Update content when official rules change
