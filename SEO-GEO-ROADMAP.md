# NextVisa SEO + GEO implementation roadmap

## Target
Build discoverability for NextVisa in Google, Bing, and AI answer engines. Primary market: Kazakhstan; local relevance: Shymkent. Rankings cannot be guaranteed.

## Phase 0 — Verify website source and deployment
- Locate the repository connected to the Netlify site `nextvis.netlify.app`.
- Confirm framework, routes, build command, and production deploy branch.
- Do not publish placeholder routes or unverified visa facts.

## Technical foundations
- Confirm HTTPS, mobile usability, performance, crawlability, and indexability.
- Add/validate robots.txt and XML sitemap; include only real canonical pages that return 200.
- Set canonical URLs, social metadata, favicon, and descriptive image alt text.
- Configure Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit sitemap.
- Add analytics and measure WhatsApp/Telegram/contact-form conversions.
- Use structured data only for verified facts: Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Service, Article. LocalBusiness only if eligible and business details are verified.

## Country landing pages
Create unique, helpful pages only after routes are implemented and facts checked against official sources:
Canada, USA, UK, Australia, Schengen, Germany, Italy, Japan, South Korea, China, New Zealand.
Each page should include audience, official-source links, checklist, application steps, official fees separated from service fees, processing-time caveats, FAQs, reviewed date, and CTA. Avoid near-duplicate pages and guarantees.

## Local SEO
- Set up or verify Google Business Profile if eligible; use truthful address/service-area details.
- Keep business name, phone, hours, and service descriptions consistent on legitimate directories.
- Ask real customers for honest reviews; never fabricate or buy reviews.
- Add authentic team/service photos and respond to reviews.

## Content and GEO
- Publish useful specific articles with clear headings, direct answers, official references, author/reviewer, and update dates.
- Answer real customer questions; don't use misleading structured data or promise rich results.
- Keep brand, service, contact, and policy information consistent across site and trusted profiles.
- llms.txt is supplementary and does not guarantee AI visibility.

## Keyword clusters
- Kazakhstan: visa assistance, visa consultation, visa application help, visa service in Shymkent, visa agency in Kazakhstan.
- Destinations: Canada, USA, UK, Australia, Schengen, Germany, Italy, Japan, South Korea, China, New Zealand.
- Tours: Australia gold prospecting tours, VIP/private tours, international tours from Kazakhstan.

## Measurement
Track indexed pages, impressions, clicks, average position, CTR, organic leads, conversion rate, and local profile actions monthly. Establish a baseline first.
