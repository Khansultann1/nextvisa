# NextVisa — 30-Day Growth Sprint

Goal: validate acquisition channels and create a repeatable qualified-lead funnel. This is an execution plan, not a promise of traffic or revenue.

## Week 1 — Foundation and offer
- Day 1: confirm actual production repository and publishing access
- Day 2: audit every page, CTA, mobile layout, contact channel, and forms
- Day 3: choose 3 initial offers: Canada, UK, Schengen (validate service capability first)
- Day 4: define service inclusions, exclusions, transparent price/quote logic, response SLA
- Day 5: prepare CRM fields: destination, purpose, target travel month, lead source, consent, status
- Day 6: configure click and form conversion events; test end-to-end
- Day 7: baseline KPI snapshot and backlog prioritization

## Week 2 — Search-ready content
- Publish only fact-checked destination landing pages
- Create one guide per destination and 3 supporting FAQ/blog pieces
- Add internal links between destination pages, guides, FAQs, and service CTA
- Write unique page titles/meta descriptions and canonical tags
- Submit sitemap and check indexing tools (Search Console/Bing Webmaster)

## Week 3 — Social distribution and trust
- Create 10 short-video scripts answering real visa questions
- Make 5 carousel/checklist posts from verified information
- Add trust elements only if genuine: team, process, real reviews with permission, service terms
- Repurpose each destination guide into a short video, carousel, and Telegram post
- Track leads and source; do not judge by views alone

## Week 4 — Conversion optimization
- Review visits, CTA clicks, form starts, submissions, qualified leads, bookings, payments
- Find the page/channel with the best qualified-lead rate
- Improve the weakest funnel step (page clarity, CTA, response speed, qualification)
- Test one change at a time and compare results
- Prepare next sprint based on evidence

## Guardrails
- No fabricated reviews or success rates.
- No guarantee of visa approval.
- Verify every fee, checklist, and timeline against official sources before publishing.
- Do not collect passport/financial documents through unsecured forms without appropriate privacy and security controls.
- Publish a clear privacy notice and service terms before collecting personal data.
