## Planning a visitor trip
Australia visitor options depend on passport, travel purpose, and eligibility. Do not assume one visitor visa pathway applies to all travelers.

## Preparation checklist
- Identify the correct visa option using official guidance.
- Clarify travel purpose, dates, itinerary, accommodation, and funding.
- Gather identity, financial, employment/family ties, and other supporting evidence appropriate to the application.
- Follow the official online application checklist; requirements vary.

## Process overview
1. Confirm visa pathway and eligibility.
2. Create/use the official application account and submit forms.
3. Upload supporting documents and pay the official fee shown.
4. Complete biometrics or health checks if requested/required.
5. Monitor official account/email for requests and decision.

## Avoid common mistakes
- Applying under the wrong visa category.
- Contradictory dates or travel purpose.
- Missing evidence or unclear funds.
- Treating processing estimates as guarantees.
