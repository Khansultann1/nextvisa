## Visitor visa basics
Travel purpose determines the visa category. Tourism and certain temporary visits may use the B-2 or combined B-1/B-2 category where appropriate. Applicants must review current official guidance and requirements.


## Preparation
- Confirm the correct category for the planned activity.
- Complete the official DS-160 form carefully and consistently.
- Prepare a concise explanation of trip purpose, itinerary, funding, and relevant circumstances.
- Follow current instructions for fee payment, interview appointment, and required documents.


## Process overview
1. Read the official visitor visa guidance.
2. Complete DS-160 and save the confirmation.
3. Pay the official application fee and follow local appointment instructions.
4. Attend the interview if required.
5. Follow the consular decision and passport return instructions.


## Avoid mistakes
- Inaccurate or inconsistent answers.
- Misrepresenting employment, finances, travel history, or purpose.
- Relying on unverified advice or promises of approval.
