## What this page covers
Tourism, visiting family/friends, and other permitted visitor activities. Eligibility and permitted activities depend on the applicant's circumstances and the current rules.

## Before applying
- Confirm whether the traveler needs a visa for their passport nationality and trip purpose.
- Check permitted activities and stay limits on GOV.UK.
- Prepare a clear trip purpose, itinerary, funding plan, and evidence that supports the application.
- Use the official document guidance; there is no universal checklist for every applicant.

## Application overview
1. Read the current Standard Visitor guidance.
2. Complete the official online application.
3. Pay the official fee shown at application.
4. Follow instructions to book and attend a visa application centre appointment, if required.
5. Track the application using the official process.

## Common preparation pitfalls
- Inconsistent itinerary or dates.
- Unclear source of funds.
- Documents that do not support statements in the form.
- Unsupported claims or altered documents.
