## Understand the short-stay visa
A Schengen short-stay visa generally concerns visits within the short-stay limit, but destination, main destination, purpose, and individual circumstances determine where/how to apply. Confirm current rules from official sources.


## Where to apply
Determine the competent consulate/visa centre using the official guidance for the itinerary and main destination. Avoid choosing an application location based only on convenience.


## Document preparation
Use the destination authority's current checklist. Typical categories may include identity/travel documents, itinerary, accommodation, travel medical insurance, financial evidence, purpose-of-trip evidence, and circumstances/ties—but the exact list and format vary.


## Process overview
1. Determine main destination and competent authority.
2. Review its official checklist and appointment rules.
3. Prepare a coherent itinerary and supporting evidence.
4. Submit the application, biometrics where required, and official fees.
5. Track status through the official channel.

## Important
Approval is not guaranteed. Do not purchase non-refundable travel based solely on an application or processing estimate.
